Overview:
In this assignment, I worked on identifying, reviewing, and hardening different services on my system. Across the weeks, I focused on improving security by disabling unsafe services, fixing SSH settings, and setting up a firewall. This summary explains what I completed and how the system became more secure.

Week 1 Summary
In Week 1, I checked all running services and found several insecure ones like Telnet and FTP. I also noticed that SSH allowed password login and root login. I disabled Telnet and planned the SSH fixes for later weeks. This week helped me understand the system’s starting security level.

Week 3 Summary  
In Week 3, I focused on the firewall and service monitoring. I enabled the firewall, allowed only important ports (like SSH), and blocked everything else. I also checked logs and stopped unused services. This made the system more controlled and reduced the attack surface.
Week 4 Summary
In Week 4, I completed all hardening tasks and reviewed everything again. I checked SSH, firewall rules, disabled services, and updated the system. I fixed small configuration issues and confirmed the system was secure. This week finalised the whole assignment.

Overall Improvements 
- Unsafe services were removed.  
- SSH was hardened (no password login, no root login).  
- Firewall was configured properly.  
- System updates were applied.  
- Logs and services were checked regularly.
